# BRINC

> BRINC builds a connected ecosystem of drones, software, and services purpose-built for public safety in the United States. Its Drone as First Responder (DFR) systems put a camera overhead within seconds of a 911 call. BRINC has no public developer API surface at this time; this file is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the company's public web presence.

## Products
- Guardian & Guardian Station: Large outdoor DFR drones (up to ~62 min flight, ~200 sq mi coverage).
- Responder & Responder Station: Mid-sized outdoor DFR drones (~28 sq mi coverage).
- Lemur 2: Indoor tactical-operations drone.
- BRINC Ball: Throwable two-way communication device.
- LiveOps: Teleoperations, livestreaming, two-way comms, and evidence management software.
- Safeguard: Hardware/software upgrade and repair/replacement program.
